Stalled Construction Project Recovery & Owner Representation

Wellington, FL · Consulting & Recovery

Overview

A home addition sat inactive for more than six months after the owner's dispute with the original contractor, who ran into COVID-era financial trouble. Rotted roof trusses added to the problem.

Our Role

CCSG stepped in as the owner's independent representative:

  • Independent construction and mold consulting
  • Construction management and oversight
  • Advice on releasing funds by milestone
  • A new concrete deck and a concrete overlay on the existing elevated deck

Outcome

The project was completed under the existing contract, avoiding litigation and the cost of a replacement contractor.
