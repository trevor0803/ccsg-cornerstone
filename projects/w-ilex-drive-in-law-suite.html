In-Law Suite Addition & Structural Carport Conversion

Palm Beach County, FL · Additions

Overview

CCSG partnered with an upscale remodeling contractor to convert an existing carport into an in-law suite.

Our Role

CCSG handled the structural scope. A roofing contractor installed the trusses, sheathing and roof, and the remodeler completed the finish work:

  • Coordinated plans and permits
  • Built the slab, footings and ICF walls
  • Cut through an existing CMU wall and modified the slab for plumbing connections

Outcome

A structurally sound in-law suite, delivered through coordination between CCSG, the roofer and the remodeler.
