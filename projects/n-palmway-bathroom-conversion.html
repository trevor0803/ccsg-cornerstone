Investment Property Renovation & Second Bathroom Conversion

Lake Worth Beach, FL · Remodel & Structural

Overview

CCSG partnered with a real estate investment group to structurally modify a two-bedroom, one-bath home. The goal was to convert an exterior laundry room into an interior second bathroom and to open the living space by removing a load-bearing wall between the family room and kitchen.

Our Role

The investment group completed windows, framing, insulation, drywall and finishes. CCSG's scope:

  • Coordinated plans and permits
  • Cut and repaired the concrete slab to relocate plumbing
  • Raised the former laundry room floor with wood joists and sheathing to match the main residence
  • Cut through an existing CMU wall and installed a concrete lintel for direct access to the new bathroom
  • Completed the structural work to remove the load-bearing wall between the family room and kitchen

Outcome

The structural work let the investment group convert the home into a more functional two-bedroom, two-bathroom property with an open interior layout.
