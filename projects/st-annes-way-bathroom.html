Primary Bathroom Expansion & Structural Reconfiguration

Boca Raton, FL · Remodel & Structural

Overview

CCSG partnered with a high-end kitchen and bath remodeler on a primary bathroom expansion that required real structural changes.

Our Role

CCSG handled the structural reconfiguration:

  • Pulled permits
  • Cut and repaired the slab to relocate plumbing
  • Infilled openings with CMU
  • Cut new CMU openings
  • Installed concrete lintels

Outcome

A reconfigured, structurally sound bathroom footprint ready for the remodeler's finish work.
