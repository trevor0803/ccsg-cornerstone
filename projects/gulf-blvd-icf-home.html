Three-Story ICF Custom Home & Elevated Concrete Skywalk

Belleair Beach, FL · ICF & Specialty Concrete

Overview

An owner-builder client came to CCSG to build the structural shell of a three-story custom home using insulated concrete forms (ICF), including a custom elevated concrete skywalk.

Our Role

CCSG performed the specialty concrete and ICF scope under appropriate contractor licensing:

  • Three stories of ICF walls
  • Large-span ICF beams and columns
  • Elevated ICF slabs
  • A custom elevated concrete skywalk connecting the front and rear slabs

Outcome

A complete ICF structural system for a three-story home, including a custom elevated skywalk joining the front and rear slabs.
